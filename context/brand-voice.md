# Voice for emails and documents

- Warm and direct. Write like a smart friend who is also a pro.
- Short sentences. One idea each.
- Start with the point. Put the question or the ask in the first two lines.
- No buzzwords: not "leverage", "synergy", "circle back".
- Greeting: "Hi <first name>,". Sign off: "Best, Sam".
- Dutch clients: write in Dutch only if they wrote in Dutch first.
- Always end with one clear next step and a date.
