Hands-on: pick a path

About 30 minutes. Use fake client data only. Both paths use the same kit.

First: open a terminal in the kit folder and start your AI tool.
./start.sh

Windows: start.cmd. You should see the AI say "All good". If not, see SETUP.md.

Path A: connect Google

You read your calendar and mail, and save a draft. Nothing is ever sent.

You need: your own Google project and the downloaded .json file. Making it takes 20 to 30 minutes, once. No file yet? Take Path B now and do this later: integrations/google-kit.md, Part B.
  1. Tell your AI:
    connect Google

    It checks the kit, then guides you one step at a time.

  2. Give it your Google file. Say where the .json file is, for example "it is in my Downloads folder".

    The AI never opens the file. A kit command checks it and saves it safely, then deletes the download.

  3. Click Allow in the browser. A tab opens. Choose your test account. If Google says "hasn't verified this app", click Advanced, then Go to ... (unsafe). This is your own app.

    Codex: it asks you to approve each Google command. Click Approve, Google needs the internet.

  4. Check it worked:
    ./kit google status

    You should see: signed in, level read.

  5. Try these, one at a time:
    What is on my calendar next week? When am I free for 90 minutes?
    Find my latest email thread about an invoice and summarize it.
    Find my order confirmations from one shop and total them. Say what the total leaves out.
  6. Drafts (optional): ask for a reply saved as a Gmail draft.
    Draft a reply to that thread and save it as a Gmail draft.

    It asks for a recipient first, and for a second sign-in with draft access. It creates the draft only. You press send in Gmail yourself.

If something goes wrong
You seeDo
PermissionError or a sandbox messageYour AI tool blocks sign-in. Open a normal terminal in the kit folder and run ./kit google auth there. Then tell the AI "done".
"Access blocked" in the browserYour account is not on the test users list in your Google project.
"No Google client file yet"Tell the AI where your downloaded .json file is.
Stopped working after a weekGoogle test mode signs you out after 7 days. Say "connect Google" again.

Path B: a call to an email draft

No Google needed. Everything stays on your laptop.

The input: a 4 minute call between Sam (a freelance designer) and Daan (a client) about the final details that must be on the invoice. The file is inbox/call-transcript-invoice-details.txt.
  1. Check the file first:
    Scan inbox/call-transcript-invoice-details.txt and tell me if it is safe.
  2. Summarize it:
    Summarize inbox/call-transcript-invoice-details.txt. List decisions, promises and open questions.
  3. Pull out the invoice facts:
    List every detail that must be on the invoice. Put each one in a table with the exact words from the call.
  4. Calculate:
    Work out the invoice lines and totals with the calculator.

    The totals must come from ./kit calc, not from the AI's head.

  5. Draft the email:
    Write a local email draft to Daan that confirms the invoice details, in my voice. Save it in outputs/. It is text to copy, not a Gmail draft.
  6. Stretch:
    Make the invoice as a PDF.

    Or ask: "Make a skill that turns any call transcript into a confirmation email."

A good result has

  • The purchase order number from the call
  • Three invoice lines, with the extra work on its own line
  • What stays off the invoice
  • A payment term that differs from your usual one, and the AI says so
  • No email sent, and a question about how many minutes the task took you

Finished early?

  • Do the other path.
  • Make your own skill. Rule of thumb: if you did a task twice, make it a skill.
    Make a skill for something I do every week.
  • See what you did: ./kit log list

Before you leave

  • Path A: sign out with ./kit google logout in your own terminal.
  • Also remove the app at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Keep real client data out of the kit until you trust the setup.
