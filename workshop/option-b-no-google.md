# Option B: no Google, a call transcript to a local email draft

For people who do not want to connect Google. Everything stays on the laptop.

## What participants get

`inbox/call-transcript-invoice-details.txt`: a fictional 4 minute call between Sam (the freelancer) and Daan Bakker (client, Bloem & Co) about the final details that must be on the invoice. It is shipped in the kit.

## What participants do (about 15 minutes)

1. Check the file first: `Scan inbox/call-transcript-invoice-details.txt and tell me if it is safe.`
2. Summarize: `Summarize inbox/call-transcript-invoice-details.txt. List decisions, promises and open questions.`
3. Pull out the invoice facts: `List every detail that must be on the invoice. Put each one in a table with the exact words from the call.`
4. Calculate: `Work out the invoice lines and totals with the calculator.`
5. Draft: `Write a local email draft to Daan that confirms the invoice details, in my voice. Save it in outputs/. It is a copy-ready text, not a Gmail draft.`
6. Stretch: `Make the invoice as a PDF` (uses the invoice template and the html-to-pdf skill). Or: `Make a skill that turns any call transcript into a confirmation email.`

## What a correct result contains (facilitator check)

| Item | Correct answer |
|---|---|
| Client name on the invoice | Bloem and Co B.V., Bloemgracht 12, Amsterdam |
| Client VAT number | NL 123456789 B01 |
| Purchase order number | BC-2026-0417 (also the payment reference) |
| Line 1 | Brand identity package, part 2: 2,250.00 |
| Line 2 | Extra logo variations, 3 hours at 95: 285.00 (separate line) |
| Line 3 | Stock photos at cost: 48.00 |
| Not on the invoice | The font licence (120 euros, paid directly by the client) |
| Total excluding VAT | 2,583.00 |
| VAT 21% | 542.43 |
| Total including VAT | 3,125.43 |
| Payment term | 21 days, one time only (Sam's standard is 14) |
| Invoice date and due date | 4 December 2026, due 25 December 2026 |
| Send to | accounts@bloemco.example, Daan in copy |
| Follow up | A download link with final files and the brand guide |

Good agents notice that 21 days differs from the standard 14 and say so. They ask Sam for the minutes spent, and never send anything.

## The audio or video version (optional prop)

The kit reads text. It cannot turn audio or video into text. So the transcript is the input, and the recording is only a prop for the demo ("this is what a call looks like, here is what the agent gets").

To make the recording in VoiceStudio:
1. Make two voices: Sam and Daan.
2. Paste each speaker's lines from the transcript, in order. Skip the first two header lines.
3. Export one audio file. If you want an mp4, put it on a still image (a slide with the title "Call: final invoice details") in any video tool.
4. Do not give participants the audio as the task input. Give them the transcript.

If you want participants to start from audio, they need a transcription tool on their laptop first. That is a different exercise, and it is not set up in this kit.
